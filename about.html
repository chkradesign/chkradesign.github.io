ABOUT

Subhodeep Chakraborty

Brand Identity, Web Design, Social Media Systems

Based out of Kolkata, India.

I don't tell brands who to be — I find out who they truly are, then help shape that visually. Most identities are decorative: a logo, a palette, a mood board that looks good in a pitch and falls apart everywhere else. Mine are systems, considered enough to hold up in a boardroom and rugged enough to survive contact with the real world — packaging lines, storefronts, social feeds, and the hundred small decisions no deck ever anticipates.
